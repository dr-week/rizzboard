# RizzBoard Dating Communication Playbook

## 1. Core Principles
- **Specificity > Generic:** Always reference a detail from their message (`extractTopicKeyword` in `rizz-engine.ts`).
- **Playful Tension (Tease):** Light teasing beats over-complimenting.
- **Low-Friction Date Pivot:** Move from texting to coffee/drinks within 4–6 messages.

## 2. Reply Archetypes
| Vibe | Goal | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Smooth** | Warm, confident, effortless | *"Was literally just thinking about texting you—great timing."* |
| **Tease** | Playful challenge or banter | *"Look who finally decided to grace my notifications."* |
| **Bold** | Direct escalation to plans | *"Skip the small talk: what is your go-to spot for drinks?"* |

## 3. Roman Konkani + English Banter
- **Smooth:** *"Borem asa! Tum koso asa?"*
- **Tease:** *"Arey, itlem busy asa ki mhaka visarlo?"*
- **Bold:** *"Choll, coffee-k vetam—kedna free asa?"*
