# RizzBoard Business & Deal-Closing Communication Playbook

## 1. Core Principles
- **Clarity & Authority:** Short, direct sentences beat long explanations.
- **Outcome-Focused:** Always anchor on the next concrete step (call, contract, timeline).
- **Calm Objection Handling:** Acknowledge the concern, reframe on ROI/value, and propose a clear next move.

## 2. Deal-Closing Reply Archetypes
| Scenario | Goal | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Follow-Up** | Re-engage without sounding needy | *"Quick check-in—are we still aligned to kick this off this week?"* |
| **Price Objection** | Anchor on value & ROI | *"Totally fair—let us look at the ROI: this pays for itself in week one."* |
| **Closing** | Lock in the next concrete step | *"If everything looks good, I can send the agreement over today—shall we lock it in?"* |
